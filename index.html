A Dare Wheel with 108 Playful Challenges

Need a quick way to get a party, sleepover, or game night moving? Tap the center of the wheel and it will randomly choose one of 108 funny dares. There is no account, download, or setup—just open the game and spin.

The challenges mix acting, dancing, impressions, jokes, creative prompts, and harmless group activities. Anyone can skip a dare that feels uncomfortable and spin again.

How to Play

  1. Gather your friends and decide who spins first.
  2. Press SPIN and wait for the wheel to choose.
  3. Try the dare, or choose a new spin if it is not right for you.

Made for Groups and Game Nights

Use the wheel as a quick party icebreaker, a sleepover activity, a game-night challenge, or a playful way to settle whose turn it is. It works in any modern mobile or desktop browser.

Looking for a particular mood? Try the party dare wheel, dare wheel for friends, sleepover dare guide, or our list of funny dares.

Example Dares on the Wheel

  • Perform a dramatic weather forecast for the room.
  • Create a handshake with the player to your left.
  • Do your best dinosaur impression.
  • Invent a ridiculous new holiday.
  • Make up a movie title starring everyone in the room.
  • Give a dramatic trailer voice-over for the next spin.

Dare Wheel Questions

Is the dare wheel free?

Yes. The full 108-dare wheel is free to play and does not require an account.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The wheel adapts to phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop screens.

Can a player skip a dare?

Absolutely. Keep the game fun and comfortable for everyone. A player can always skip a challenge and spin again.

Will the wheel choose the same dare twice?

Each spin is random, so repeats are possible. With 108 choices, there is plenty of variety for a group game.

Use the wheel with friends, be kind, and choose a new spin anytime.
