Spin a Sleepover Dare

Turn “What Should We Do?” into a Game

Sleepovers often have a moment when snacks are open, everyone is still awake, and nobody wants to choose the next activity. A random dare wheel solves that small problem: press spin, read the result aloud, and let the challenge start the next round of conversation and laughter.

The wheel works without an app or account. Put it on a phone for a small group or mirror a laptop to a larger screen so everyone can watch the pointer stop.

Sleepover Dare Ideas

  • Tell a spooky story in exactly three sentences.
  • Try to draw a cat with your eyes closed.
  • Talk to a pillow as if it is your celebrity crush.
  • Create a dance inspired by a household chore.
  • Pretend you are a sleepy bear waking up.
  • Make a song out of three random words.
  • Act like a detective solving a missing-sock mystery.
  • Pretend to host a cooking show with no ingredients.

These prompts work especially well indoors because they rely on imagination and performance instead of supplies. The complete wheel contains many more options for a longer game.

Simple Sleepover Rules

  1. Let each person spin once per round.
  2. Give everyone an unlimited right to skip, swap, or soften a challenge.
  3. Do not photograph, record, message, or post anything without clear permission.
  4. Keep noise, movement, food, allergies, and the space around you in mind.
  5. Stop the game when it is no longer fun for the group.

These rules leave room for the surprising part of a dare game while keeping every player in control of their own participation.

Try a Themed Round

Impression round

Keep spinning until each person receives an acting, character, or animal impression.

Creative round

Use results involving songs, stories, drawings, inventions, advertisements, or imaginary products.

Group round

Adapt a selected dare so everyone participates together—for example, turning a solo dance into a ten-second group dance.

Sleepover Dare Questions

Do we need any supplies?

No supplies are required for the wheel. A few prompts may mention an ordinary safe object, but players can always respin.

Can we use only indoor dares?

The wheel includes a few outdoor prompts. If going outside is not appropriate, skip those results and spin again.

Can we use the wheel more than once?

Yes. Spin as many times as the group wants. Each result is random, so an occasional repeat is possible.
