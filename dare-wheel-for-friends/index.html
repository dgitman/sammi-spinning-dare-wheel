Spin with Friends

Make an Ordinary Hangout More Playful

The dare wheel is designed for the moment when friends want to do something together but nobody has a game ready. It opens in a browser, works on a shared phone or larger screen, and starts with a single button. The challenges invite the group to perform, improvise, move, tell jokes, or invent something silly.

Friends can play in a circle, pass one device around, or nominate the next spinner after each round. There are no scores to track and no account to create, so the game is easy to join and easy to stop.

Three Ways Friends Can Play

Take turns

Go around the group in order. Each person spins once and completes or skips the selected challenge.

Pick the next player

After finishing a dare, choose which friend spins next. Make sure everyone gets included before someone receives a second turn.

Play as teams

Split into two groups. Each team can earn a point for attempting a dare, with no penalty when someone chooses to respin.

Fun Dares for a Group of Friends

  • Create a handshake with the player to your left.
  • Make up a chorus for a silly song.
  • Act out an emoji chosen by the group.
  • Give a compliment to everyone in the room.
  • Create a cheer for the person to your right.
  • Rank the group’s best animal impressions.

The full wheel adds dozens of acting, storytelling, movement, and imagination challenges. For a comedy-focused round, browse the funny dare ideas before playing.

Keep the Game Friendly

A random result should create a laugh, not pressure. Agree before the first round that every person may skip or modify a dare. Avoid recording or posting someone without permission, make space for different abilities, and choose a safe alternative whenever the room or activity calls for one.

If the group includes people who do not know each other well, begin with impressions, storytelling, and creative prompts. Save more performative challenges for later, after everyone understands the tone of the game.

Playing with Friends: Questions

Is this a truth or dare wheel?

This wheel selects dares only. It does not ask truth questions, so every result is an activity or creative challenge.

Can we play without downloading an app?

Yes. The game runs directly in a modern web browser and requires no installation or signup.

Are the results random?

Yes. Each spin chooses a result from the full list of 108 dares, so the same dare can occasionally appear again.
